# What users complain about in other expense apps → what Msarifi does

| # | Complaint | Sources | Msarifi v2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data lost on reinstall / failed restore | ReviewPilot (Money Manager), Trustpilot (Spendee), r/androidapps (1Money) | Backup reminder after 14 days, Export through the share sheet (WhatsApp/Drive), navigator.storage.persist() |
| 2 | Recurring transactions broken, no end date | justuseapp & appsupports (Money Manager) | Monthly recurring rules with an end month, pause, edit; a deleted occurrence never comes back |
| 3 | Manual entry is tedious | r/ynab, finantres (1Money) | Frequent presets, note autocomplete, the note picks its past category, ?add=1 Back Tap |
| 4 | Ads, paywalls, price hikes | ReviewPilot, r/ynab price evolution, AppWereld (Monefy), r/copilotmoney | Free, no ads, no account |
| 5 | Import/export behind premium | r/BudgetBakers | JSON export/import free |
| 6 | Sync failures / forced sign-outs | App Store (Wallet), AppWereld (Monefy) | No account/sync by design |
| 7 | Hard to learn / busy UI | ReviewPilot, r/copilotmoney, r/ynab (Goodbudget) | Simple screens, Darija |
| 8 | No period comparison or useful breakdowns | r/copilotmoney (Monefy) | Insights: vs last month to date, biggest increase, top category share, savings rate |
| 9 | No proactive warnings | r/copilotmoney | Safe-to-spend/day until payday, run-out date, budget pace, upcoming bills |
| 10 | No per-category budgets | AppBrain (Monefy) | Per-category budgets (already) |
| 11 | Category not auto-picked from note | AppBrain (Monefy) | Done |
| 12 | Privacy worries (clipboard/files) | r/ios, justuseapp (Money Manager) | Local only, eye toggle, optional PIN |
